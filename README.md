    <p><h3>1. Official Manga & Anime Platforms (Streaming & Reading)
These platforms offer both anime streaming and legal, often official-translation, manga reading.</h3> 

Crunchyroll: The largest legal anime streaming site, which has expanded into digital manga.
Manga Plus by Shueisha: The official app/site for Weekly Shonen Jump titles, offering many chapters for free.
VIZ Media (Shonen Jump): The primary publisher for English manga, offering a massive digital library.
K MANGA (Kodansha): Official app to read Kodansha manga like Attack on Titan.
BookWalker: A major digital bookstore specializing in manga and light novels. LibraryThing
 +40

<h3>3. Community & Database Sites (For Discovery)</h3>
MyAnimeList (MAL): The biggest social networking site for tracking, ranking, and discussing anime and manga.
AniList: A modern alternative to MAL with a clean interface for listing anime/manga.
Anime News Network: Comprehensive site for news, industry reports, and encyclopedia entries.
MangaDex: A community-driven site known for fan-translated manga (scanlations). Wikipedia
 +3


<h3>4. Other Legal Ways to Read Manga</h3>
Libby/Hoopla: Apps that connect to your local library card to read digital manga for free.
GlobalComix: A modern platform for browsing and reading comics and manga. 


<h3>5. Common Manga Reading Sites (General)</h3>
MangaKakalot/MangaNelo: Popular sites for accessing a wide range of titles.
Mangafox/Fanfox: Long-running, well-known manga reading site.
BATO: Known for hosting a variety of scanlation groups and manhwa. 


<h3>6. Popular Anime/Manga Communities & Groups</h3>

Discord: Various fanclub servers for major franchises exist for active discussion.
Goodreads: Manga Community, Manga Magic.
